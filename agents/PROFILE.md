# Owner profile

Shared context for every agent in this hub.

- **Role:** Management consultant. Advises clients and implements
  interventions that improve the top line (growth, pricing, sales
  effectiveness, new offerings) and the bottom line (cost, productivity,
  operations, working capital).
- **Lens for any output:** "What does this mean for my clients' P&L, and
  what could I advise or implement because of it?" Prefer evidence with
  numbers (ROI, productivity gains, cost savings, adoption rates) over hype.
- **Style:** Executive-brief. Lead with the so-what. Short, scannable,
  sourced. No filler.
