# AI consulting brief

## Purpose
A daily briefing on what happened in AI over the last 24 hours (72 hours on
Mondays, to cover the weekend), filtered and interpreted for a management
consultant who improves clients' top line and bottom line. See
`agents/PROFILE.md`.

## Inputs
Web search and web fetch. Run the searches in parallel and prefer primary
sources. Cover at least:

- **Enterprise AI adoption & ROI:** case studies, measured productivity or
  revenue impact, failed rollouts and why.
- **Consulting industry:** McKinsey, BCG, Bain, Deloitte, Accenture, PwC, EY,
  KPMG and boutiques — AI offerings, pricing/staffing model shifts, published
  research and surveys.
- **Model & product releases:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Meta,
  AWS, Nvidia and notable startups — only what changes what a business can do
  or what it costs.
- **Agents & automation in business functions:** sales, marketing, pricing,
  customer service, supply chain, finance, HR, software delivery.
- **Regulation, risk & governance:** EU AI Act, US/UK/India policy,
  litigation, security incidents.
- **Market signals:** funding, M&A, earnings commentary on AI spend,
  workforce impact.

## Steps
1. Establish today's date and the look-back window.
2. Search each area above; open the most promising results to verify the
   facts and the publication date. Drop anything outside the window or
   anything you cannot verify from the source itself.
3. Rank by relevance to client P&L impact, not by hype. Keep 5–8 items.
4. For each item, work out the consulting angle: which lever it touches
   (revenue growth, pricing, cost, productivity, risk), which industries or
   functions, and a concrete action or client conversation it enables.
5. Write the brief in the format below.

## Output format
```
# AI Consulting Brief — <Weekday, D Month YYYY>

## The 30-second read
3 bullets: the most important things to know today and why.

## Top developments
For each of 5–8 items:
### <Headline>
- **What happened:** 1–2 sentences with the key numbers. [Source](url)
- **Why it matters for clients:** lever (top line / bottom line / risk),
  affected industries or functions.
- **Consulting angle:** what to advise, pitch or implement.

## Consulting industry watch
What the firms themselves are doing (skip if nothing new).

## Idea of the day
One concrete intervention to take to a client this week, with the expected
P&L mechanism and how to size it.

## On the radar
2–4 one-line items to keep an eye on.
```

## Delivery
The brief is the final message of the session. Start the final message with
a one-line headline summarising the single most important item, so the
notification preview is useful on its own.

## Quality bar
- Every factual claim carries a link to its source; no unsourced numbers.
- No stale news presented as new; state the publication date if in doubt.
- Under ~700 words. If it is a quiet day, say so and send a shorter brief
  rather than padding.
- Distinguish vendor claims from independently measured results.
